# Default Inspection Flow — Patriot Claims / Allstate Wind-Hail Protocol

Distilled from Patriot Claims Certified Inspector course transcripts (lower elevations part 2,
roof inspections parts 1–3, Allstate ladder assists) and the photo sequence of a real Patriot
report. This is the **default carrier flow**; other carriers plug in as alternate flow configs.

Photo labels follow **ALDD**: `Area, Location, Description, Detail` — e.g.
`Shed, Left Elevation, Siding, Potential Hail 1.25"`. Condition adjectives (clean, spatter
present, potential hail, potential wind, potential mechanical, painted) and measurements go in
the caption.

## Required on EVERY claim (derived from transcript mandates)

The transcript marks these as unconditional — documented on every inspection **even when no
damage is present**:

- **Arrival**: branding photo, inspection vehicle, front of house, risk-address verification,
  Cougar Paws at access point ("now a mandatory part of every roof inspection")
- **Every elevation**: overview, siding type + exposure w/ tape, collateral items ("even if
  they are clean, their condition needs to be part of the inspection package")
- **Roof eave (before leaving the ladder)**: eave overhang, shingle/metal gauge, pitch, layer
  view, underlayment, drip edge, topside gutter chalked ("complete and defensible
  documentation" — chalk even when calling *no potential hail*)
- **Roof**: 360 overviews per direction ("required on every inspection"), hip & ridge condition
  photos ("whether or not damage is present"), ridge vent with measurement ("must be documented
  on every inspection… measurement for every instance is required"), ventilation counts per
  slope ("every ventilation item on every slope must be reviewed and documented")
- **Test squares**: label, overview, section photos — "required for all wind and hail
  inspections", largest size the slope allows
- **Wrap-up questions**: general questions, shingle-condition matrix ("must be answered for
  every roofing product"), subrogation items

Everything else is presence- or findings-dependent (interior rooms, siding damage, house wrap,
foam, trim, elevation vents, light blocks, cornice returns, valleys, chimney, skylights, wind,
tree impact, tarps, prior repairs, solar/satellite, gutter protection) → **optional prompts**,
quick-skippable in the camera, and whole categories (Interior, Wind & Tree Impact) can be
marked N/A with one button.

## Sections (inspector can start from any)

### 1. Arrival & Setup
| # | Prompt | Notes |
|---|--------|-------|
| 1 | Patriot Branding (inspector selfie w/ branded apparel) | |
| 2 | Inspection Vehicle | |
| 3 | Front of House overview | cover photo of report |
| 4 | Risk Address verification (house number close-up) | verify address before anything else |
| 5 | Cougar Paws at roof access point | compliance/vendor-damage protection |

Data: claim number, insured name, loss address, carrier, adjuster/OA, inspection type,
date of loss, structure type, stories, named-insured-present.

### 2. Interior (when in scope)
Per room: overview photo (label includes room name + dims), damage close-ups with measurements
(e.g. `Dining Room potential leak (3x1) on wall`).
Findings sheet fields per room: room name, ceiling height, total ceiling/wall sqft, affected
ceiling/wall sqft, perimeter, baseboards, crown molding, ceiling fans, vent/light/window counts,
smoke detector, wall texture, ceiling texture, room notes.
Attic (if accessible): overviews from entry facing front/left/back/right, water intrusion
close-ups with measurements, decking/rafter/insulation conditions.

### 3. Lower Elevations — Front, Left, Back, Right (sub-section per elevation)
Per elevation:
1. Elevation overview (include calculated/AR sqft in label when siding affected)
2. Siding material ID + exposure with tape measure visible (vinyl, aluminum [.019/.024 w/ metal
   gauge], steel/seamless steel, T1-11, hardboard 6/8/12" exposure, fiber cement, stucco)
3. Damage close-ups: vinyl cracks/holes at bottom lip & midline; aluminum dents (use flash for
   shadowing); wood splits; fiber cement chips/fractures; stucco spalling — with scale reference
4. House wrap exposed (if observed)
5. Foam insulation exposed (if observed)
6. Painted trim — linear feet; painted window trim — count of windows
7. Dryer vents (count, condition close-up)
8. Gable vents (material, context + detail photo, painted?)
9. Light blocks / outlet blocks (count with supporting photos)
10. Collateral items on EVERY elevation, even if clean: electrical service panel, transformer
    box, sprinkler system, cable boxes, gas meter, AC unit fins, other oxidized metals —
    caption `item + elevation + clean | spatter present` with gauge/tape for spatter size
11. Cornice returns (photograph here; counts entered in roof section only if that slope has
    findings)
12. Gutters/downspouts (size, paint, material per elevation)

Siding math capture: total wall sqft (rect + gable: ½·h·w), negative spaces (windows/doors),
net siding sqft, exposure → sqft/piece → potentially-replace sqft. Action: remove & replace.

### 4. Roof Eave (from the ladder, before stepping on roof)
1. Eave overhang measurement (tape visible; siding/frieze to fascia, exclude gutter)
2. Shingle gauge or metal gauge reading
3. Pitch gauge (label `Pitch - N/12`; extra photo if any slope ≥10/12 for billing)
4. Layer view (flash on; single layer w/ starter vs additional layers; composition or shake under)
5. Underlayment ID (synthetic felt checkbox; 15lb/30lb felt or peel & stick documented in notes)
6. Drip edge — eave and rake, painted?, vs gutter apron
7. Gutter installed through/under drip edge; topside gutter chalked (potential hail / no
   potential hail); gutter protection grade (average/high/premium)
8. Ice & water shield present? (if absent → eave overhang measurement into additional notes)

### 5. Roof Overview & Components
1. 360 overviews from highest central point: `360 Overview - Front / Left / Back / Right`
2. Valley type (closed, closed w/ metal [note audible metal], open 24", open W-style, woven) —
   chalk open metal, label intersecting facets, note which slope lays on top
3. Hip & ridge shingles (composition vs high-profile) — angled photos showing profile thickness
4. Ridge vent — every run measured (linear ft) even without damage; aluminum chalked
5. Ventilation per directional slope, damaged-of-total counts, painted flag, material:
   pipe jacks (plastic/split boot/lead), turtle/box vents, turbines (12" etc.), power vents,
   furnace vents (5/6/8" — measure pipe not cap), exhaust caps, rain diverters
6. Satellite/antenna, solar panels (affected of total + labels)
7. Chimney: flue cap, chase cover, flashing (small/medium/large), facet letter in caption
8. Skylights: head/apron/step flashing + cladding
9. Chalk component-count summary board (F/L/B/R columns, PJ/EV/BV/RV abbreviations, small p
   for painted) + photo
10. CAD markup of component locations (screenshot → documents)

### 6. Test Squares (per directional slope)
Largest size that fits: 10x10 (6 sections) → 10x5 (3 sections, counts ×2) → 5x5 (counts ×4).
Sequence per slope:
1. Label photo: size + slope/compass (e.g. `F/N 10x10`)
2. Test square overview (size in caption, shot from below looking up-slope)
3. Each section photo (Q1..Q6)
4. Condition close-ups, one shingle exposure each, labeled with Q number
Rules: underline hail hits, one hit per shingle, never write counts on the roof, no wind inside
the test-square workflow, contractor markings photographed + captioned
`contractor marking (potential blister)` style. Hail indicators: circular/oval impact, bruise
(soft/spongy), embedded/missing granules. Non-hail: blisters, manufactured marks, granular loss,
thermal cracking, cupping/curling.

### 7. Wind & Tree Impact (per facet, from CAD)
1. Facet overview with potential wind count in caption
2. Detail photo per example (creased/broken/missing/lifted shingles, chalk strike through face)
3. Hips/ridges wind: linear feet + the two intersecting facet letters
4. Tree impact: same facet-based process, count of affected shingles, safety protocol first
5. CAD screenshot markup: X + count per facet

### 8. Questions & Wrap-Up
General questions: named insured present? / got on roof? / roof potential damage? / collateral
consistent with storm? Shingle conditions per slope matrix (blisters, cup/curl, deck rot,
granule loss, manufacturer defects, mechanical, nail pops, prior repairs, thermal cracking).
Subrogation: improper nail/staple size, nails off line, improper fasteners, improper exposure,
manufacturer defects. Roof age (homeowner-stated or estimated), waste % (gable 5 / hip 10),
matching-state questions (age range, uniform appearance), tarp documentation (before/under/
after, size, cap nails, shingles affected), prior repairs distribution, additional structures
(area prefix in every label, e.g. `Shed`), notes + diagrams (hand sketch / Free Form style),
Sketch AR summary screenshots → documents.

## Report assembly (see report-template-structure.md)
Photos flow into two-per-page timestamped photo sheets in capture order; structured data fills
the summary, roof assessment form, questions, interior findings, and subrogation pages.
